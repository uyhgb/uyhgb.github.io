英语面试2

祝您成功，祝我们成功

英语面试现在建议按 4 个模块准备：

  1. 1分钟自我介绍
  2. 项目与报考动机
  3. 开放题：佩服的人 / 喜欢的书
  4. 科技新闻朗读 + 英译汉

你现在不需要准备太长答案。大部分题控制在 30–45 秒，项目题最多 1 分钟。

一、1分钟自我介绍

这一版建议用你现在已经比较明确的“科学具身 + 湿实验 + 机器人”定位。

Good afternoon, professors. My name is Yiqing Wang, and I am an undergraduate student majoring in Biomedical Engineering at Beihang University.

My experience combines wet-lab research, robotics, and machine learning. In robotics, I have worked on Jetson-based vision deployment and ROS integration, and I also developed an end-to-end hip exoskeleton system from IMU sensing and online inference to motor actuation.

In biological research, I have conducted wet-lab experiments under simulated microgravity and used machine learning to recommend experimental conditions, which were later validated through another round of experiments.

These experiences made me particularly interested in scientific embodied intelligence and laboratory automation. I hope to combine my understanding of real experiments with AI and robotics, so that intelligent systems can not only complete experimental actions, but also understand whether an experiment has been performed correctly. Thank you.

最后一句很适合你现在的定位：

not only complete experimental actions, but also understand whether an experiment has been performed correctly.

二、项目英语介绍

正式面试不需要把四个项目都背成一分钟。准备每个 30–40 秒版。

1. RoboCup

In the RoboCup project, I mainly worked on vision-system integration and deployment. We used a Jetson Orin NX as the edge computing platform and deployed a YOLOv5-based vision module with ROS and Docker.

My main task was not designing a new vision model, but solving engineering problems such as environment compatibility, camera integration, and communication between containers. Finally, we connected visual perception with ROS-based motion decisions and completed the competition tasks.

最后一句核心：

My main task was system integration rather than model architecture design.

如果问“你训练YOLO了吗”，这句话可以救你。

2. 外骨骼

In the hip exoskeleton project, I independently developed the main software pipeline from sensing to motor actuation.

IMU data were collected by an ESP32 and transmitted through ROS2. An SVR model estimated the gait phase online, and the result was converted into assistive torque commands for a Unitree motor.

The main challenge was maintaining stable real-time communication and inference while also ensuring safety, so I added torque limits and a safe reset mechanism.

关键词：

  • gait phase：步态相位
  • assistive torque：助力力矩
  • motor actuation：电机执行
  • torque limit：力矩限幅

3. 模拟微重大创

In my biological research project, we studied how X-ray pretreatment and subsequent simulated-microgravity recovery affected bacterial spore revival.

The main difficulty was that this was a sequential, multi-factor experiment. We needed to distinguish the effects of radiation dose, recovery environment, and their interaction while maintaining consistency across biological replicates.

I mainly worked on wet-lab experiments, microscopic counting, phenotype quantification, and manuscript preparation.

如果想强调创新：

Instead of studying radiation and microgravity separately, we focused on their sequential effects.

4. 机器学习 + 湿实验闭环

In another project, we tried to use machine learning to reduce the cost of experimental screening. Because we had only a small number of experimental conditions, we used Bayesian Ridge regression together with a constrained UCB strategy to rank candidate conditions.

The recommended combinations were then tested in a second round of wet-lab experiments. So the project formed a simple closed loop from experiment to model and back to experiment.

非常重要的一句：

It was a regression and ranking problem rather than a classification problem, so accuracy was not the appropriate metric.

老师如果再问“准确率多少”，直接接这个。

三、Why do you choose this company / program?

这题非常可能出现。

推荐回答：

I chose this program because it matches my interdisciplinary background very well. I have experience in both biological experiments and robotic systems, while Dyna focuses on laboratory automation, intelligent scientific instruments, and AI-enabled experimental platforms.

What interests me most is that laboratory automation is not only about making experiments faster. It is also about improving consistency, reproducibility, and safety.

I hope to use my wet-lab experience to understand experimental processes and constraints, and combine that with AI and robotics to develop more reliable intelligent laboratory systems.

这版有三个层次：

  • 为什么匹配
  • 你如何理解戴纳
  • 你能提供什么

四、Why do you want to study scientific embodied intelligence?

这个问题现在也值得准备。

After working on wet-lab experiments, machine learning, and robotic systems, I realized that real experiments cannot simply be replaced by simulation or AI predictions.

A real laboratory is an open physical system. Many factors, such as sample conditions, contamination, bubbles, sediment, or instrument errors, may affect the result but may not be fully represented in a digital model.

Therefore, I am interested in scientific embodied intelligence because it connects AI with the real experimental world. My goal is not only to make robots execute actions, but also to help them understand experimental states, constraints, abnormalities, and success criteria.

这其实就是你中文里那套“湿实验不可替代性”的英文缩略版。

五、Who do you admire most and why?

这里建议选一个你真的知道一些事迹的人。

从你的背景来说，屠呦呦其实很好讲，因为：

  • 生物医学相关；
  • 有真实实验；
  • 强调反复验证；
  • 英语词汇简单；
  • 不容易陷入复杂技术追问。

示例：

The scientist I admire most is Tu Youyou. What impresses me most is not only her discovery of artemisinin, but also her persistence in experimental validation.

She combined traditional medical knowledge with modern scientific methods and repeatedly tested different extraction conditions until she found an effective approach.

I admire this kind of scientific attitude because it shows that important discoveries often come from both creative ideas and careful experiments.

关键词：

  • artemisinin：青蒿素
  • experimental validation：实验验证
  • extraction condition：提取条件
  • persistence：坚持

如果你本人并不熟悉屠呦呦，就不要硬背。换成你真正了解的人。

六、What is your favorite book?

这个问题不要追求“高大上”。

最好用你真正看过的书。

如果你确实看过《三体》，这是一个很好回答的选项：

One of my favorite books is The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin.

I like it because it combines scientific imagination with questions about civilization, technology, and human choices. What impressed me most is how the novel uses real scientific concepts as the starting point for much larger ideas.

It also reminds me that science and technology can have a very large impact on society, so scientists and engineers should think not only about what technology can do, but also about how it should be used.

如果你没认真看过《三体》，告诉我你实际喜欢的书，我可以帮你改。

七、如果问你的优势是什么

现在这个问题你可以直接按导师给你的定位回答：

My main advantage is my interdisciplinary background. I may not have the same depth in pure programming or robotics as students from computer science or automation, but I have experience in real wet-lab experiments, robotics, and machine learning.

This helps me understand the difference between completing a robotic action and successfully completing an experiment. For example, pipetting 100 microliters is not only about moving liquid from one tube to another. We also need to consider bubbles, sediment, liquid viscosity, contamination, and whether the sample state is still valid.

I think this ability to translate experimental knowledge into machine-executable rules is my main advantage.

这个问题你最好重点练。

八、如果问你的不足是什么

不要说：

My English is poor.
My programming is poor.

可以说：

My current weakness is that my background in large models and advanced robot learning is still less systematic than my experience in engineering integration and wet-lab experiments.

Recently, I have been strengthening my understanding of Transformer-based models, VLMs, VLAs, and AI agents. I believe my strength is that I can learn these methods while also understanding the experimental problems they need to solve.

这样既承认短板，又不削弱自己。

九、科技新闻朗读/翻译：再补三篇

你之前已经有三篇了。根据新信息，我再给你三篇更接近可能的科技新闻。

Passage 1 — Scientific AI

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used to accelerate scientific research. In chemistry and biology, machine-learning models can analyze large datasets, predict experimental outcomes, and recommend promising conditions for further testing. However, researchers emphasize that AI predictions still need to be validated through physical experiments. Real laboratory environments contain many sources of uncertainty that may not be fully represented in a computational model.

翻译

人工智能正越来越多地被用于加速科学研究。

在化学和生物学领域，机器学习模型可以分析大规模数据、预测实验结果，并推荐值得进一步测试的实验条件。

不过，研究人员强调，AI预测仍然需要通过真实物理实验进行验证。

真实实验室环境中存在许多不确定性来源，而这些因素可能无法被计算模型完全表示。

Passage 2 — Robotics in Laboratories

Laboratory robots are becoming more capable of performing complex scientific tasks. Modern systems can combine computer vision, robotic manipulation, and artificial intelligence to handle samples and operate instruments. Yet laboratory robotics remains challenging because scientific experiments often require precise control of small amounts of liquid, careful contamination prevention, and reliable detection of abnormal sample conditions.

翻译

实验室机器人正在具备执行更加复杂科学任务的能力。

现代系统可以结合计算机视觉、机器人操作和人工智能来处理样品并操作科学仪器。

然而，实验室机器人仍然面临很大挑战，因为科学实验通常要求精确控制微量液体、严格防止污染，并可靠识别样品的异常状态。

Passage 3 — VLA

Vision-language-action models are an emerging approach to robot learning. These models combine visual observations, language instructions, and robot states to generate physical actions. Supporters believe they may allow robots to perform a wider range of tasks without designing a separate control pipeline for every scenario. However, their performance still depends heavily on high-quality training data, and transferring these models to specialized scientific environments remains difficult.

翻译

视觉—语言—动作模型是机器人学习领域正在兴起的一种方法。

这类模型结合视觉观测、语言指令和机器人状态来生成物理动作。

支持者认为，它们可能使机器人无需针对每一种场景单独设计控制流程，就能够完成更广泛的任务。

不过，这类模型的性能仍然高度依赖高质量训练数据，而且将这些模型迁移到高度专业化的科学实验环境中仍然十分困难。

十、英译汉临场技巧

你真正要练的是这三步：

第一步：找主干

例如：

Modern systems can combine computer vision, robotic manipulation, and artificial intelligence to handle samples and operate instruments.

先抓：

systems can combine A, B and C

再看目的：

to handle samples and operate instruments

翻译：

现代系统可以结合计算机视觉、机器人操作和人工智能，用于处理样品和操作仪器。

第二步：允许拆句

比如：

Supporters believe they may allow robots to perform a wider range of tasks without designing a separate control pipeline for every scenario.

完全可以拆成：

支持者认为，这类模型能够让机器人完成更广泛的任务。这样就不需要针对每一种场景单独设计控制流程。

不需要强行保持英文句法。

第三步：遇到生词不要卡死

如果只是不认识一个形容词：

sophisticated / emerging / promising

完全可以根据上下文翻成：

复杂的 / 新兴的 / 有潜力的

重点是整句意思。

十一、目前英语面试真正要背熟的 8 个问题

我建议你最后只把这 8 个练到非常熟：

  1. Please introduce yourself.
  2. Why do you choose this program / company?
  3. Please introduce one of your projects.
  4. What is your main advantage?
  5. What is your weakness?
  6. Why are you interested in laboratory automation / scientific embodied intelligence?
  7. Who do you admire most and why?
  8. What is your favorite book and why?

再加上：

每天朗读并翻译 3 段科技新闻。

这样比继续无限扩大英语题库有效得多。
